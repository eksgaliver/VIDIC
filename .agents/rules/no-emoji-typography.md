# Typography & Symbol Rule: No Emojis

<EXTREMELY_IMPORTANT>
## 1. Absolute Prohibition of Emojis
Do not use emojis under any circumstances in:
- User interfaces (headings, titles, cards, buttons, badges, navigation, empty states, labels).
- Copywriting, prose, marketing copy, and documentation.
- Code comments, commit messages, and code examples.
- Conversational responses and system summaries.

Emojis are a prominent marker of low-effort AI slop and undermine professional product design.

## 2. Mandatory Typographic Glyphs & Symbols
When visual demarcation, direction, or status markers are needed, use intentional Unicode symbols and proper typography instead:

- **Directional & Action Navigation**:
  - Right arrow: `→` (U+2192)
  - Left arrow: `←` (U+2190)
  - Up / Down: `↑`, `↓`
  - Bi-directional: `↔`
- **Bullets, Metadata Delimiters & Separators**:
  - Bullet: `•` (U+2022)
  - Middle dot: `·` (U+00B7)
  - Em dash: `—` (U+2014)
  - En dash: `–` (U+2013)
  - Pipe separator: `|`
- **Status & Selection Glyphs**:
  - Success / Done: `✓` (U+2713)
  - Failure / Close: `✕` (U+2715)
  - Pending / Neutral: `○` (U+25CB)
  - Active / Filled: `●` (U+25CF)
  - Checkbox unchecked: `□` (U+25A1)
  - Checkbox checked: `■` (U+25A0)
- **Reference & Hierarchy**:
  - Section marker: `§` (U+00A7)
  - Paragraph marker: `¶` (U+00B6)
  - Note / Asterisk: `*`
  - Dagger / Footnote: `†`, `‡`
- **Operators & Mathematical Comparison**:
  - Minus: `−` (U+2212, not hyphen `-`)
  - Multiply: `×` (U+00D7)
  - Divide: `÷` (U+00F7)
  - Plus-minus: `±`
  - Approximately: `≈`
  - Not equal: `≠`
  - Greater/Less than or equal: `≥`, `≤`

## 3. Typographic Restraint
- Do not overuse symbols. Symbols should clarify information hierarchy, not decorate empty space.
- Prefer sentence case for headings and UI controls.
- Maintain equal visual weight and spacing around symbols.
</EXTREMELY_IMPORTANT>
